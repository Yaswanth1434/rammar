Note: These are examples, not endorsements, verified suppliers, or RAMMAR partners. Product offerings vary by country. RAMMAR does not provide or forward manufacturer contact details.

What to include when contacting a manufacturer

  1. Your country or service region and the product category.
  2. The application, operating environment, and what you need the product to do.
  3. The exact model or part number, if you are replacing or matching existing equipment.
  4. Relevant requirements such as voltage, load, dimensions, accuracy, certifications, or compatibility.
  5. Whether you need product guidance, a local authorized distributor, a quote, technical support, installation, training, or maintenance.

Do not send passwords, payment-card details, patient information, or other sensitive data through general inquiry forms. For safety-critical, electrical, or medical applications, consult an appropriately qualified professional.
